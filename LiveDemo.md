# Live demo test plan

## Trustchain music profile

### 1. Launching the app
- Click on the 'Music' tab.
- Home screen should show at least two pre-defined albums.

### 2. Liking songs
- Select the 'Night Cruising' album.
- Like the song 'Morning Call'.
- Go to the leaderboard and confirm the song appears there.
- Click the song in the leaderboard to go back to the album view.
- Unlike the song in the album.
- Return to the leaderboard.

### 3. Tagging songs
- Like 'Morning Call' again.
- Add a tag to the song.
- Check the leaderboard to confirm the song is liked.
- Unlike the song.
- Return to leaderboard.

## Ethereum artist payout

### 4. Donating
- Navigate to the 'Donation' page.
- Enter an Ether amount for donation.
